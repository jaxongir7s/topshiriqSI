Multiple Linear Regression · Interactive lab

Predict car prices and see the math behind it

Upload a car dataset, watch the model being built step by step with the normal equation, and then estimate the price of any car. Everything runs in your browser, in vanilla JavaScript.

Every row is one car. The last numeric column whose name contains price is the target (what we predict); all other numeric columns are features (what we predict it from). You can switch features on or off below.

The model is ŷ = β₀ + β₁x₁ + β₂x₂ + … + βₖxₖ. Least squares picks the β values that minimise the squared errors, and the normal equation gives them in closed form: β = (XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀy. Open each step to see the real matrices computed from your data.

How well does the fitted model reproduce the prices it was trained on? Points close to the dashed diagonal mean accurate predictions. Residuals should scatter randomly around zero.
